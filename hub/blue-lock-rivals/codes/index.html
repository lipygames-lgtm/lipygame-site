Where Blue Lock: Rivals codes come from

Blue Lock: Rivals is a 5v5 soccer game on Roblox, inspired by the Blue Lock anime. It belongs to the Blue Lock Rivals Unofficial Fans group, which has over 32 million members. It was created on July 26, 2024, and was close to 5 billion visits as of September 28, 2026. The game's own description says it's fan-made and not official.

Codes are posted on a Trello board that presents itself as the game's official one, on a card called "Codes" that separates active codes from expired ones. Sites that track the game also mention the codes channel on the official Discord (discord.gg/bluelockrivals), which you can only read with an account. The game's Roblox description doesn't list codes, and the game's official links only show up for people signed in to Roblox. That's why we mark each code as confirmed by independent sources, not as official.

In our October 8, 2026 check, the active codes were DONREVAMP, SENDOUNEXT and ACEEATER, from the October 3, 2026 update. The four codes from the previous update, the Snuffy one (which brought a new Master Class style), have already expired: the game's Trello, updated on October 5, and the sites RoCodes, Beebom, Destructoid and PCGamesN agree. The game's codes are short-lived: SNUFFYSOON, from the previous batch, came out on September 19 and expired on the 26th, according to RoCodes.

What are Lucky Style Spins and Lucky Flow Spins?

In Blue Lock: Rivals, your Style is your playstyle: a set of abilities inspired by a character from the anime, like Isagi, Nagi, or Rin. Flow is an extra power you activate when your Flow bar fills up, and it gives you a bonus for a while. Both are rolled with spins.

Codes give lucky spins: Lucky Style Spins to roll a Style, and Lucky Flow Spins to roll a Flow. According to that Trello board, a lucky spin makes you more likely to get a Legendary than an Epic.

Rarities range from Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Mythic up to World Class and Master Class. Even with a lucky spin, Master Class, Snuffy's tier, is extremely rare: sources put the odds well below 1% but don't agree on the exact number.

How we check

Before a code goes on the list, it's checked against the official source (when the game publishes its codes) or against two independent sources. We show where each one comes from:

  • Official sourcepublished by the game itself or by its studio.
  • Confirmed by 2 sourceslisted as active by independent sources that don't copy each other.

A code with only one source, a conflicting status, or the look of a recycled list stays out. Players' "it worked" and "didn't work" votes help us quickly pull codes that have stopped working.

Channels checked: Official Blue Lock: Rivals page on Roblox, Trello board that presents itself as the game's official one ("Codes" card), Group that owns the game on Roblox (Blue Lock Rivals Unofficial Fans).

Frequently asked questions

Why didn't the code work?
Check three things: that you're already level 10, that you've joined the Blue Lock Rivals Unofficial Fans group, and that you copied the code exactly, with the capital letters. A new code sometimes fails on a server still running the old version of the game: leave and rejoin. Codes generally work once per account. If it still fails, tap "didn't work" to let other players know.
Why isn't SNUFFYSOON among the active codes?
Because that Trello board already lists it as expired, and Destructoid, GamesRadar, Beebom, and RoCodes say the same. Some outdated sites still showed it as active when we checked, and codes with a conflicting status don't go on the active list here. It's on the expired list.
Is Blue Lock: Rivals the anime's official game?
No. The game's Roblox description says it's fan-made and unofficial, and that it wasn't supervised or approved by the creators of Blue Lock or by Kodansha, the manga's publisher. It also says the game is allowed under the License Manager, Roblox's licensing system.
How often do new codes come out?
No set day has been announced. Judging by past releases, codes arrive with game updates, and some last only a few days, so it's worth redeeming them as soon as they appear.
Is there a code for Robux or a guaranteed Snuffy?
No. Blue Lock: Rivals codes give spins: Lucky Style Spins, Lucky Flow Spins, and sometimes event spins. Anyone promising Robux, a guaranteed Snuffy, or infinite spins in exchange for your login is running a scam.
