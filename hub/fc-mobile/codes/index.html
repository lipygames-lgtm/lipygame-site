Where FC Mobile codes come from

EA shares codes on FC Mobile's official channels (X, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Twitch, and its WhatsApp channel), on FC Mobile LIVE streams, and at promotional events. The official September 2026 codes we found were posted on X by @EASFCMOBILE, always with a link to the redemption site.

They usually tie in with soccer and in-game news: the return of the Premier League (CLEANSLATE), the Bundesliga (AUFGEHTS), and the Süper Lig (BOMBAGOL), plus the FC Mobile 27 update (27UPDATE), which arrived on September 24, 2026, along with the 3rd anniversary event.

Why isn't my code working?

  • You signed in to the site with a different EA Account: it has to be the same one that's linked to the game.
  • The game has no linked EA Account (you're playing as a guest): link one in Settings > Account Management > Link Accounts.
  • The code expired: every FC Mobile code has a deadline, and after that it can't be redeemed.
  • You already used it: most codes work once per account.
  • Typo: copy and paste instead of typing.
  • The reward didn't show up: wait a few minutes and check your in-game Inbox.
  • Underage account: under EA's rules, players below the minimum age in their country can only play as guests, and child accounts can't sign in to EA websites. In that case, redeeming on the site won't work.

Why do other sites show more codes?

Because many lists keep months-old codes marked as "active." When we checked on September 28, some sites still showed July and August codes as active, even though other sources already listed them as expired.

That's why a code only goes on this list if EA published it on its official channels or independent sources confirm it's active. Older codes go on the expired list, so you don't waste your time.

Common FC Mobile scams

  • FC Points, Coins, or Gems generators: they don't exist. According to EA, the only way to get FC Points is to buy them in the game or on the official FC Mobile Web Store.
  • Coin selling: it's against the game's rules and can get you suspended or banned. And sellers need your login, so your account ends up at risk.
  • Sites that ask for your password to "unlock" a code: the only place to redeem is redeem.fcm.ea.com, with EA's own sign-in. EA says it never asks for your password.
  • Codes that require downloading an app, filling out a survey, or signing up: with a real code, you just copy it and paste it on EA's site.
  • Buying "mule" accounts and prearranged trades between accounts: these are also against the rules and can get you penalized.

Frequently asked questions

What's the website for redeeming FC Mobile codes?
The official site is redeem.fcm.ea.com. You redeem in your browser, signed in to the EA Account linked to the game. Be wary of any other address.
Can I redeem a code inside the game?
Not through the official route: EA says to redeem on redeem.fcm.ea.com. In the game, you just link your EA Account and, after redeeming, claim the reward in your Inbox.
Do the codes work in my country?
The codes posted on FC Mobile's global account (@EASFCMOBILE) don't include any notice of country restrictions, and EA's redemption rules don't mention regions: the limits are the expiration date and how many times each account can use a code.
Can I use the same code on two accounts?
Each account can redeem it once. Under EA's rules, most codes can only be used once per account, and there's a limit of one offer per person.
How long does a code last?
Every code expires, but the official posts we saw don't give an end date. Based on the dates logged by sites that track the codes, they usually last from a few days to about two weeks. Redeem as soon as one comes out.
Is there a free FC Points or Coins generator?
No. According to EA, the only way to get FC Points is to buy them in the game or on the official Web Store. Any site, app, or video that promises free Points, Coins, or players in exchange for your login is a scam.
Can buying Coins from other sites get me in trouble?
Yes. Buying Coins from third parties is against FC Mobile's rules and can lead to a suspension or ban. On top of that, the seller needs your login, which puts your account at risk.
Why are some codes under "May have expired"?
Because the sources disagree on them. In our September 28 check, ZLATANWORLDPREMIERE and DOMINOSFC showed up as active on some sites and as expired on others, and we couldn't find either one on the official FC Mobile X account. That's why they appear in that section, below the active ones. The active list only includes codes from an official source or confirmed by independent sources.
