Who we are

Lipy Hub is part of Lipy Games, a mobile game studio

LHBy Lipy Hub Team·Updated September 28
In short

Lipy Hub is an independent site by Lipy Games, a Brazilian studio that makes mobile games. We bring together checked codes (each with its source), tools that work, and guides for people who play on their phones, in six languages.

Who's behind it

Lipy Games is the games brand of Lipy Serviços Digitais LTDA, a Brazilian company registered under CNPJ 65.260.227/0001-11. We make mobile games like Quiver: Arrow Puzzle, This Level Hates You, Nitrovenant, and SWARMORIA.

The Hub was born from the same team: people who make games and play every day.

Why we built the Hub

Anyone looking for a code or a tip on their phone usually lands on recycled lists full of months-old codes, or on pages that promise free items in exchange for a login. We wanted a place players could trust.

That's why every code shows the time of its last check and the source it came from, and every tool only offers what really works in the game.

How we work

  • A code only goes on the list if it has an official source or is confirmed by independent sources. We show the time of the check.
  • When there's no active official code, we say so instead of making up a list.
  • We don't publish modded APKs, hacks, cheats, or item "generators."
  • We never ask for your login, password, or account details.
  • We fix things fast: if something is wrong, let us know through the contact page.

Independent of game companies

Lipy Hub has no official connection to the companies behind the games it covers. Game names and trademarks belong to their owners and appear here to identify the games and illustrate guides and lists.

In six languages

Portuguese, English, Spanish, Arabic, Hindi, and Japanese: the same commitment Lipy Games has made since its first game. Each language gets what's true for its audience. A code that only works on the Brazil server, for example, shows up only in Portuguese until we've checked the other regions.
