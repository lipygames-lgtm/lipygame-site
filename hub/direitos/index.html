In short

How we use trademarks and images

Lipy Hub is independent and unofficial. Game names, logos, characters, and images belong to their owners and appear here only to identify the games and illustrate guides, lists, and reviews, always with credit.

We follow the fan content policies of the companies that publish them, and we don't use trademarks that companies reserve for official use.

How to request removal

  • The address (link) of the page with the content.
  • Which work or trademark is yours and where it appears on the page.
  • A way for us to reply to you.

Timeframe

We remove the content within 2 business days of the request and, if needed, discuss it afterward. Email bluetubeoficial@gmail.com.
