BusbloomFree Bus Puzzle Game

Relaxing parking puzzle

Busbloom is a parking puzzle. Each vehicle has an arrow and only moves forward. Tap it: if the path to the edge is clear, it drives out and stops at one of the boarding spots.

In the line, passengers wait their turn. The first one in line boards the vehicle of the same color and shape waiting at a spot. When the vehicle is full, it honks, drives off and frees the spot. If two vehicles of the same color are waiting, the one that arrived first fills up first.

There are only four spots. Calling a vehicle too early takes up a spot you will need later. The trick is to watch the line and take the vehicles out in the right order.

There are 36 levels in six neighborhoods: Square, School, Beach, Park, Fair and Station. Each neighborhood brings something new: vans and bigger buses, mystery vehicles, flower beds, numbered vehicles and garages. Every level you win lights up something in the neighborhood.

Gameplay video

If you like Bus Jam, Parking Jam, Car Out

If you like games where you clear cars out of a parking lot and fill buses by color, you will feel right at home. The difference is that here every level can be solved with the four spots, undo is free and each color has its own shape.

How to play

  1. Tap a vehicle to make it drive in the direction of its arrow.
  2. If the path is clear, it drives out and stops at the first empty spot.
  3. Passengers board the vehicle of the same color and shape on their own.
  4. A full vehicle drives off and frees the spot.
  5. Empty the parking lot and the line to win.
  6. Use no more than the minimum number of spots to earn three stars.

Controls

  • Mouse: click the vehicle
  • On your phone: tap the vehicle
  • Undo (or Ctrl+Z): takes back your last move, for free
  • Restart (or R): starts the level over
  • Hint, Tow and Spot: helpers that unlock from levels 4, 8 and 13

Tips

  • Look at the next passengers in line before you tap: call the vehicles of those colors first.
  • A van carries 6 and a bus carries 10. A bus sitting in a spot takes a while to fill up.
  • If a vehicle will not move, the one in front of it blinks: take that one out first.
  • A mystery vehicle shows its color once the space in front of it is clear.
  • The number on the ice-blue vehicle tells you how many vehicles must leave before it.
  • Stuck? Undo is free and does not cost a star.

What's in the game

  • 36 different levels, all checked by a program that solves each one with 4 spots
  • Six neighborhoods, each with a new rule
  • Free, unlimited undo, with no timer and no lives
  • Each color has its own shape, for players who have trouble with colors
  • Three stars for using the fewest spots possible
  • The town blooms with every level you win
  • 6 languages

Frequently asked questions

Is Busbloom free?

Yes. You play for free, right in your browser.

Do I need to download it?

No. You play on this page, with nothing to install and no sign-up.

Are any levels impossible?

No. Every level was solved by a program using the four regular spots, and the line has exactly the passengers that fit in the vehicles of each color.

What if I tap the wrong one?

Use Undo. It is free, has no limit and does not cost you a star.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. If you leave in the middle, the level comes back exactly where you stopped.
