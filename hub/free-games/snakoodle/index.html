SnakoodleFree Snake Puzzle Game

Relaxing snake puzzle

Snakoodle is a snake puzzle. Each snake takes up a few squares on the board and faces one way. When you tap it, it slithers forward in the direction of its head, and its body follows the same track.

If the path to the edge is clear, the snake leaves the board. If another snake or a rock is in the way, it bumps into it and goes back to its spot. Nothing bad happens: a bump only costs you a star.

Each chapter brings something new: turn arrows on the ground that change the snake's direction, burrows that take the snake from one spot to another, and rocks that never move.

Every snake you free goes to your garden, which gets new plants with each level. Finishing a chapter unlocks a new pattern for the snakes.

Gameplay video

If you like Twisty Snake Out, Snake Escape, Slither Out

If you like games where you clear snakes off the board, you will feel right at home. The difference is that here there is no timer and no hearts, each chapter adds a new rule, and every level has a guaranteed solution.

How to play

  1. Tap a snake to make it slither in the direction of its head.
  2. If the path to the edge is clear, it leaves the board.
  3. If it hits something, it goes back and counts as a bump.
  4. Hold your finger on a snake to see its path before you tap.
  5. Get all the snakes out to beat the level.
  6. Win without a single bump to earn three stars.

Controls

  • Mouse: click a snake; hold the click to see its path
  • On your phone: tap a snake; hold your finger to see its path
  • Undo: brings back the last snake that left (Ctrl+Z on the keyboard)
  • Restart: starts the level over (R key)
  • Hint: shows a snake with a clear path (H key)

Tips

  • Start with the snakes whose heads face the nearest edge.
  • Before tapping a snake in the middle, hold your finger on it and check that the path is green.
  • On turn arrows, follow the path with your eyes: the snake changes direction at each arrow.
  • In burrows, the snake comes out of the burrow of the same color and keeps going in the same direction.
  • Taking out one snake never gets in the way of another, so there are no dead ends.
  • Every three-star win gives you one more hint.

What's in the game

  • 40 levels in 4 chapters: Veggie Patch, Turns, Burrows and Rocks
  • Every level checked by a program that solves each one
  • No timer and no lives
  • Undo and restart as much as you like
  • Daily snake, the same for everyone, with a day streak
  • A garden that grows and 7 patterns for the snakes
  • 6 languages

Frequently asked questions

Is Snakoodle free?

Yes. You play for free, right in your browser.

Do I need to download it?

No. You play on this page, with nothing to install and no sign-up.

Are any levels impossible?

No. Every level was checked by a program before it went into the game, and taking out one snake never blocks another.

Can I earn money playing?

No. Snakoodle is just a game, with no cash prizes and no withdrawals.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. If you leave in the middle of a level, it comes back just as it was.
