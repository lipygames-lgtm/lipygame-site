TrickwinkFree Trick and Riddle Game

Trick puzzle in scenes

Trickwink is a game of tricky scenes. Each level shows a situation with a short goal at the top, like "Wake Leo up" or "Put out the candle, no blowing". To solve it, you play with the objects in the scene: drag, tap, hold, shake, rub or draw a path.

The tricks are fair. Anyone who needs something shows a speech bubble with the idea, objects that react wiggle when you tap them, and any reasonable gesture works: a curtain opens whether you drag or tap it. When you win, "The trick" card explains in one sentence why it worked.

There are 36 levels: 30 in three chapters (Home, Park and School) and 6 extras that appear as the Trick of the day. Each chapter has marked bonus levels and hard levels. The cast is friendly: Leo, Mia, Kai, Nina, Zed the zombie who is afraid of the dark, Toto the dog and Bip the robot.

There is no timer, no lives and no losing. A wrong move only makes the object wiggle, and nothing is lost. Your progress and the scene in progress are saved on your device.

Gameplay video

If you like Brain Test, Brain Out, Tricky Puzzle

If you like trick and riddle games where you have to think outside the box, you will feel right at home. The difference is that here the trick is fair: the bubble shows what the character wants, any reasonable gesture works and every solution is explained.

How to play

  1. Read the goal in the yellow banner at the top.
  2. Look at the scene: the character's bubble shows what they want.
  3. Tap, drag, hold or shake the objects to solve it.
  4. Got it wrong? Nothing bad happens: try another idea.
  5. When you win, read "The trick" and go on to the next level.

Controls

  • Mouse: click, drag and hold the button
  • On your phone: tap, drag and hold with your finger
  • Shake: drag the object quickly up and down
  • Restart: starts the scene over, for free
  • Hint: free clue, highlight and show me (free after 1 and 2 minutes)
  • Esc: back to the level map

Tips

  • Take the goal literally: sometimes the answer is in the title itself.
  • Look inside and under things before answering a counting question.
  • Think about the real world: shade cools you down, fire needs air, running makes wind.
  • If nothing seems to work, wait a bit without touching anything: one level asks for patience.
  • Even the on-screen buttons can be part of the trick.
  • Stuck? The clue is free, and the highlight and show me become free over time.

What's in the game

  • 36 scenes, each with a different trick
  • Every level beaten by a test robot without using a hint
  • No timer, no lives and no losing
  • "The trick" card explains each solution
  • Three levels of hints that become free over time
  • Sticker album and a count of tricks found without hints
  • Trick of the day with a day streak
  • 6 languages

Frequently asked questions

Is Trickwink free?

Yes. You play for free, right in your browser.

Do I need to download it?

No. You play on this page, with nothing to install and no sign-up.

Is there a level that is impossible or only opens with a hint?

No. Hints never unlock anything: a test robot beats all 36 levels without opening a hint, and a program checks that no scene has a dead end.

What if I get stuck?

Open the Hint. The clue is always free. The highlight becomes free after 1 minute in the level, and show me after 2 minutes. After 3 minutes, a skip button also appears.

Is it good for kids?

Yes. The humor is gentle, there is no timer and no jump scares, and the texts are short.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. If you leave in the middle, the scene comes back just as it was.
