Nitrovenant 2 Guide: Cars, Bosses, Tricks and Modes

Nitrovenant 2 guide with controls, all 12 cars, upgrades, nitro, combo, tricks, bosses, the Inferno and modes. Numbers checked in the game.

Play Nitrovenant 2 free now

This guide collects what you can check inside Nitrovenant 2 itself: garage numbers, boss rules, what changes in each range of stages and the extra modes.

The race is simple. You drive on three lanes, the missile launcher on the roof fires on its own and a single blue tank powers both the nitro and the machine gun. The rest is picking your lane, keeping the combo going and deciding when to spend the tank.

Controls: keyboard and mobile

On a computer, every action has its own key. On mobile, you use gestures on the road and the NITRO and MG buttons on screen.

A jump lasts less than a second. If you press a key mid-jump, the command is queued for a moment and fires as soon as the car lands. In the air, the same keys do tricks.

  • A and D or the left and right arrows: switch lanes. In the air, they do a barrel roll.
  • W, up arrow or Space: jump. In the air, W or the up arrow does a frontflip.
  • Hold R or N: nitro on.
  • Hold J, F, S or the down arrow: machine gun on. Holding the mouse button still on the road works too. In the air, S or the down arrow does a backflip.
  • P or Esc: pause the race.
  • On mobile, swipe sideways to switch lanes and up to jump. In the air, swipes do tricks.
  • On mobile, hold the NITRO button for nitro, and the MG button, or a finger held still on the road, for the machine gun.

Garage cars: from the Rook to the Titan

The garage has 12 cars, all bought with credits earned in races. Each step up adds 14 HP and 3 damage over the previous car.

Nitro grows with the car too. A full tank deals 2,000 damage plus 1,000 per garage step. That's 2,000 on the Rook and 13,000 on the Golden.

Upgrades: Weapon, Armor and Traction

Each upgrade goes up to level 8. The price of the next level is the base cost times that level's number, so the start is cheap and the end is expensive.

Maxing out all three costs 23,400 credits: 9,000 on Weapon, 7,920 on Armor and 6,480 on Traction. That's less than the price of the Nomad.

Upgrades apply to all of your cars. When you switch cars, they all stay active.

Each Traction level removes about 6.5% of the slowdown from dirt and mud. At level 8, you lose roughly half of what you would lose without it.

An order that tends to pay off is Armor and Weapon up to level 4, then the next car. Traction comes in once the mud starts getting in your way.

Credits come from zombie kills, and the combo multiplies what each kill pays. Moving on to new stages pays more than replaying the same one.

Nitro, machine gun, combo and Fury

The nitro and the machine gun draw from the same blue tank. You only spend it while you hold the button, and releasing stops it instantly.

A full tank gives you 3 seconds of nitro or 6 seconds of machine gun. Each blue can you pick up refills 25%.

With nitro on, the car goes 60% faster, becomes invincible, runs over regular zombies and pulls in the good items from all three lanes. It also damages everything in front of it, bosses included.

The combo goes up with each back-to-back kill, with a 1.2-second window between them. It reaches ×2 at 10 kills, ×3 at 25, ×4 at 50 and ×5 at 80. Taking a hit resets the combo.

Hold ×5 for 5 seconds and FURY kicks in: for 6 seconds, 50% more damage and faster shots.

Skill fills the tank too: a close call past an attack or obstacle without getting hurt, a clean landing after a pothole and reaching ×5.

Air tricks, field cards and transformations

A normal jump fits one trick. On ramps you can chain several. A clean landing fills your nitro; landing while still spinning costs a little HP and slows the car for a moment.

From the mid boss on (stage 10), the road offers 3 field cards. The race doesn't stop: you have 10 seconds to pick, or the first one is chosen for you. The card lasts until the end of the stage.

Sometimes, from stage 7 on, the car turns into a BULLDOZER, TANK or FIRE TRUCK for a few seconds. The TANK blows up groups with its cannon and takes half damage.

  • Barrel roll: spins sideways, switches lanes and fires a fan of missiles.
  • Frontflip: lands with a shockwave across the lane.
  • Backflip: the mid-value trick, good for closing out a chain.
  • Double corkscrew: two barrel rolls in a row to the same side.
  • TORNADO: a barrel roll and a flip on the same takeoff, with three or more tricks.
  • Cards: Side Saws, Mines, Magnet, Shield, Drone and +2 missile rows for the stage.

How to beat the bosses

There are four bosses: The Guard, Pit Boss, The Pilot and Head Mechanic. They take turns across the stages, in that order.

From stage 10 on, the Foreman shows up as a mid boss halfway through the stage, and the final boss comes at the end. The mid boss doesn't use bombs.

Every attack comes with a warning: the lane turns red before the hit. Get out of it. The boss never uses the same attack twice in a row.

From stage 4 on, each boss gets a signature move on top of the standard attacks.

After any charge, the boss backs off and is dazed for 2 seconds. During that time it doesn't attack and takes double damage. That's the moment to spend your nitro or machine gun.

From stage 35 on, the final boss may bring a parachute drop with an ally car that fights by your side.

Stages, worlds and the Inferno: tips for each road

There are 120 stages across Sunny Fields, Scorched Desert, Rain Forest, Frozen Pass and Dead City, plus the Inferno.

The ground changes within each stage. There's asphalt, a stretch of dirt, asphalt again, a stretch of mud and asphalt to the finish. Dirt cuts your speed by 10% and mud by 20%.

From stage 6 on, stages get types: Night Run, Horde, Fog, Rescue and Minefield. Each one explains its rule the first time.

From stage 45 on, losing a race drops your car into the Inferno. Survive to the end to get back to your stage. From stage 50 on, the same race can drop you into the Inferno twice.

Each stage is worth up to 3 stars: win, hit the stage's combo goal and finish with 70% HP or more.

  • Stage 5: kamikazes arrive. Jump or switch lanes when they mark yours.
  • Stage 10: the mid boss and field cards show up.
  • Stage 18: the final boss starts throwing bombs.
  • Stage 45: losing sends you to the Inferno.
  • Stage 50: double the kamikazes and two trips to the Inferno per race.

Modes: Daily Challenge, Boss of the Week and Endless

The Daily Challenge unlocks after stage 3. It's a new road every day, with a modifier, and pays a prize for your first win of the day. It doesn't affect your stages or stars and never drops you into the Inferno.

Boss of the Week pays credits and an exclusive paint for your first win of the week. The boss changes on Monday.

Endless mode unlocks at stage 10. There's no boss and no finish line: it gets harder every few hundred meters, and the goal is to beat your distance record.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car in Nitrovenant 2?

The Golden, with 254 HP, 45 damage and the strongest nitro at 13,000. It costs 800,000 credits, so the way there is to climb the garage step by step.

Is it better to upgrade your car or buy a new one?

Early on, upgrade. Upgrades are cheap, apply to every car and, all together, cost less than the Nomad. After that, each new car adds HP, damage and more nitro damage.

How do you beat the boss without losing much health?

Get out of the red lane before every attack and learn each boss's signature move. After a charge, it's dazed for 2 seconds and takes double damage: save your nitro for that moment.

How long does the nitro last and how do I recharge it?

A full tank lasts 3 seconds of nitro or 6 of machine gun. Each blue can refills 25%, and clean landings, tricks and close calls top it up a little too.

How does the Inferno work?

From stage 45 on, losing a race drops your car into the Inferno. If you survive to the end, you go back to your stage. From stage 50, this can happen twice in the same race.

Does Nitrovenant 2 have ads or purchases in the browser?

No. In the browser the game has no ads and no purchases: you earn everything by racing. Coming soon to Android.
