3D legion: action and strategy

LEGIONTIDE complete guide

LEGIONTIDE is a 3D legion game: you lead an army down a track and pick the gates that add, multiply or change your troop type. Only the troops that go through the gate count.

What sets it apart is picking the right army for each fight. Before every clash, the enemy shows its size and what beats it: mages against swarms, archers against exploding mushrooms, giants against armored foes. At the end of the track, a giant boss is waiting, with attacks marked on the ground and a weak spot.

Between battles, you look after an island kingdom: crown your monarch, build structures that make your troops stronger and defend the wall from skeleton hordes. The kingdom keeps making gold even when the game is closed.

Gameplay video

If you like Count Masters, Mob Control, Crowd City

If you like Count Masters, Mob Control or Crowd City, you'll feel right at home: the gates multiply your army the same way. The difference is that here the type of troop matters. Shieldbearers, archers, giants and mages each beat different enemies, every track ends with a giant boss, and between levels you build your own kingdom.

How to play

  1. Steer the legion toward the gates that grow your army the most.
  2. Squeeze the formation into a thin column to slip past hammers, saws, boulders and arrow rain.
  3. Before each fight, check the enemy's strength and send in the right troops.
  4. Use each class's power and the Golden Legion at the right moment.
  5. In the arena, dodge the boss's attacks and hit its weak spot.
  6. Take the gold back to your kingdom and power up your troops.

Controls

  • Mouse: move to steer the legion
  • A / D or arrow keys: steer the legion
  • Hold the left mouse button (or Shift): squeeze the formation into a column
  • 1, 2, 3 and 4: class powers (Shield Wall, Arrow Rain, Ground Slam, Arcane Blast)
  • Space: Golden Legion (when the bar is full)
  • Esc: pause
  • On mobile: drag to steer, hold to squeeze the formation and tap the power buttons

Tips

  • Not every gate helps: some take troops away. Check the number before you go through.
  • Read the warning before each fight: it shows the enemy's size and which troop beats it.
  • Shield bearers block enemy arrows. Keeping a few up front protects the rest of the legion.
  • In the Mushroom Forest, archers take out the exploding mushrooms from a distance.
  • Save the Golden Legion for a tough moment: your army becomes invincible for a few seconds.
  • In the boss fight, every attack is marked on the ground. Get out of the marked area.
  • Finish on the staircase, the gold wall or the cannon to multiply your gold.

What's in the game

  • 30 levels, 10 in each biome
  • 3 biomes: Green Fields, Mushroom Forest and Frozen Peaks
  • 6 bosses, two in each biome
  • 5 troop types, 4 powers and the Golden Legion
  • Island kingdom with buildings, offline gold and skeleton defense
  • 6 languages: Portuguese, English, Spanish, Hindi, Japanese and Arabic

Frequently asked questions

Is LEGIONTIDE free?

Yes. It's free and has no purchases.

Do I need to download it?

No. You play right in your browser, on this page, without installing anything.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, in your phone's browser, with touch controls. You can play in landscape or portrait. There's no Google Play version yet.

How many bosses are in the game?

There are 6 bosses, two in each biome: Orc Brute, Stone Golem, Mushroom King, Forest Warlock, Yeti and Blizzard Ghost.

What is the kingdom for?

The gold from each run goes to your kingdom, where you build structures that make your troops stronger. Every kingdom you conquer becomes an island.

Will there be more biomes?

The fourth kingdom, the Volcano, already shows up on the map, locked and marked as coming soon.
