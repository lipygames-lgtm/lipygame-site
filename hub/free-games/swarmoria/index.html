About this game

SWARMORIA: Consume & Evolve is a 3D swarm game. You guide the Core and a swarm of robots follows. Anything smaller than the swarm gets taken apart, and every object turns into more robots.

Midway through a match, three mutation cards show up, and one tap decides how the swarm grows this time. Combine two or three, and the same level becomes a whole new game.

In Titan Hunt mode, you face Titans that seem impossible at first. Your swarm grows, rips off their armor piece by piece and exposes the core. At the end of each match, your matter goes to the Nexus, a base that grows with permanent upgrades.

If you like Hole io

If you like Hole io and games where you swallow everything to grow, SWARMORIA brings the same feeling, only with a swarm of robots. The more you consume, the bigger the swarm grows, and mutations change how you play all the way to the fight against the Titans.

How to play

  1. Drag your finger to move the Core. The swarm follows.
  2. Touch objects smaller than the swarm to devour them.
  3. Every object becomes more robots. Grow until you can reach the bigger objects.
  4. Pick one of the three mutation cards when they show up.
  5. At the end, spend your matter in the Nexus to get stronger for the next match.

Controls

  • On a computer: drag with the mouse to move the Core and click to pick a mutation
  • On mobile: drag to move the Core and lead the swarm
  • On mobile: tap to pick a mutation card

Tips

  • Start with small objects: each one grows the swarm and opens up the bigger ones.
  • Object too big for now? Come back to it once the swarm has grown.
  • Replicator helps you grow fast; Armored makes the swarm lose fewer robots to hazards.
  • Magnetic pulls in matter from far away, so nothing gets left behind.
  • Against the Titan, consume the arena and grow before the first attack.
  • Get out of the Titan's counterattack zones: they wipe out robots.
  • Invest your matter in the Nexus: the upgrades are permanent.

What's in the game

  • 2 modes: Assimilation Run and Titan Hunt
  • 3 Titans, one at the end of each world
  • 6 mutations: Replicator, Corrosive, Rapid, Armored, Magnetic and Salvager
  • Nexus with permanent Replication, Mobility, Resistance and Recovery upgrades
  • 4 swarm skins: Base, Void, Nano and Plasma
  • 2- to 4-minute matches, played with one finger
