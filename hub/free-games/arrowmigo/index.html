About this game

Arrowmigo is an arrow puzzle. Each arrow takes up a few squares on the board, straight or with turns, and its tip shows where it goes. When you tap an arrow, it slides forward, its body follows the tip and it leaves through the edge.

An arrow only leaves if the path from its tip to the edge is clear. If another arrow is in front, it bumps and goes back to its spot. A bump does not make you lose anything: it only costs a star. Taking out one arrow never gets in the way of the others, so there are no dead ends.

The challenge is spotting which arrow is free in the middle of the tangle. Holding your finger on an arrow shows its path before you tap.

Chapters have 10 levels each. Each chapter brings something new (turns, rocks that never leave and ice that only melts when a neighbor leaves) and ends with a level shaped like an animal's silhouette. Beating that level frees the friend for your album of 30.

If you like Amaze GO!, Arrows Puzzle Escape, Tap Away

If you like games where you clear arrows off the board, you will feel right at home. The difference is that here there are no lives, no timer and no leagues, each chapter brings a new rule and you free a little animal at the end of each chapter.

How to play

  1. Tap an arrow: it moves in the direction of its tip.
  2. If the path to the edge is clear, it leaves the board.
  3. If another arrow or a rock is in front, it bumps and goes back. That counts as a bump.
  4. Hold your finger on an arrow to see its path.
  5. Take out all the arrows to win. With no bumps, the level is worth three stars.
  6. An iced arrow only leaves after an arrow touching it has left.

Controls

  • Mouse: click the arrow; hold the click to see its path
  • On your phone: tap the arrow; hold your finger to see its path
  • Keyboard: arrow keys choose, Space or Enter fires
  • Hint: lights up a free arrow (H key)
  • Restart: starts the level over (R key)

Tips

  • First look at the arrows on the edge that point outward.
  • Arrows with the longest paths are the hardest to spot.
  • When in doubt, hold the arrow: the path appears and the obstacle is marked in red.
  • On ice, start with the arrows touching the frozen arrow.
  • Rocks never leave, but no arrow depends on a rock to get out.
  • The free daily hint points to the free arrow with the longest path.

Frequently asked questions

Is Arrowmigo free?

Yes. You play for free, right in your browser.

Do I need to download it?

No. You play on this page, with nothing to install and no sign-up.

Can I lose a level?

No. A bump only costs a star. If you turn on Focus Mode in Settings, each level has 3 drops and, when they run out, the level starts over.

Does every level have a solution?

Yes. Every level goes through a program that removes the arrows one by one and checks that the board ends up empty.

Is my progress saved?

It is saved in this browser. In Settings there is a backup code to keep and restore your progress.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The whole board always fits on the screen, and a tap picks the arrow closest to your finger.
