The feeling

There's a moment when traffic closes in from both sides, Freeze snaps on — and the whole world drops into slow motion while your line of pets crosses behind you. That moment is the game.

How to play

Hop. Rescue.
Freeze.

01

Hop between the cars.

Every tap is one tile. Wait for a gap in the traffic, the trains and the river logs before you hop, and always check both sides of the road.

arrows, WASD or swipe
02

Rescue the pets.

Lost pets join the line behind Hoppy. The longer the line, the bigger the risk and the reward. Get them all to the rescue van to save them for good.

the line grows with every rescue
03

Use Freeze.

Saving pets fills the Freeze bar. When traffic gets tight, it slows the whole world for a few seconds. Save it for the train.

Shift or E · full bar
04

Tend the farm.

The pets you save move to the farm. Between runs, plant, water, harvest and serve customers. A forgotten pet gets sad.

drag · tap the tool
