The studio

We are
Lipy Games.

A Brazilian mobile game studio. Small on purpose, obsessive by nature: every level is designed, tested and fine-tuned until it feels like it always existed.

We believe a great mobile game takes three seconds to understand and years to forget. That’s why we start with the feeling — the jolt, the laugh, the “so close!” — and only then write the first line of code.
