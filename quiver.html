There’s a magic second in Quiver: when you see the right order and the whole board cascades away right in front of you.

Two modes

Race the clock.
Or take your time.

RUSH

Against the clock

200 timed levels. Make a mistake? The clock speeds up. In the final seconds, every tap becomes a ticking bomb — and combos fire up FEVER mode.

  • 200 levels
  • Combos trigger FEVER
  • A clock that speeds up
CLASSIC

No rush

160 levels across 5 arenas, with hearts instead of a clock. At the end of each arena, a boss shows up to attack your board.

  • 160 levels
  • 5 arenas
  • Hearts instead of a timer

Power-ups & extras
