How to play

Simple to play.
Impossible to trust.

01

Three buttons. Zero trust.

Move left, move right, jump. The controls are simple on purpose: the difficulty lives in the scenery — in the floor that vanishes, the cloud that shoots lightning, the door that turns into a monster.

← → · jump
02

Died? You’re back in an instant.

No game-over screen, no waiting. And on the next try, everything happens exactly the same way: the level doesn’t cheat, it’s just mean. Pay attention and you’ll get through.

instant restart
03

Learn the joke. Laugh at it. Beat it.

Every level is a new joke: setup, betrayal, death and lesson learned. There are 66 levels, and no trap ever repeats.

66 levels · 0 repeats

The scenery lies

Nothing here is what it seems.

The floor turns into a mouth. The door bites. The fluffy cloud shoots lightning. A ninja shows up on your second jump. And that boulder was just waiting for you to stand still.

3 worlds · 22 levels each

to suffer.

World 1Grassy Grievances

Question blocks, suspicious bridges and a blue sky that doesn’t deserve your trust.

World 2Molten Meltdown

Real lava, fake lava and a volcano with a terrible sense of humor.

World 3Frostbitten Fury

Slippery floors, icicles with bad intentions and snowmen with a past.

Soundtrack

The music hates you too.

Every track is named after a chart-topper — with a hateful twist: Blinding Hate, Shape of Hate, I Will Always Hate You… The songs themselves are originals written for the game, and every bit as catchy. Hit play and hear a snippet of each one.
