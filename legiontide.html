One soldier is nothing.A legion is a tide.

Pick the right gates, rally shieldbearers, archers, giants and mages, and send the right army into every fight. At the end of the road, a giant boss is waiting.

The feeling

There's a moment when your legion hits the ×3 gate, the whole road turns blue — and the boss at the end suddenly looks small. That moment is the game.

How to play

Choose. Multiply.
Conquer.

01

Pick your gate.

Gates add, multiply or change your troop type. Only troops that march through count, so steer the legion to the right side.

+15 · ×2 · −11 → +2
02

Slip past the traps.

Hammers, saws, rolling boulders and arrow rain. Hold to squeeze the formation into a thin column and slip through the gap.

hold = squeeze · release = spread
03

Send the right army.

Before every clash, the enemy shows its size and what beats it. Mages against swarms, archers against exploding mushrooms, giants against armored foes.

enemy strength shows before the fight
04

Topple the boss.

In the arena, every boss attack is marked on the ground. Dodge, hit the weak spot and finish on the stairway, the gold wall or the cannon to multiply your gold.

weak spot · final blow

Troops and powers

Every troop,
a role.

Your legion is everyone who made it through the gates. Each class fights its own way and has a power for the perfect moment — keys 1 to 4 on a keyboard, a single tap on mobile.

Soldiers

The backbone of the legion. Many, fast and always on the front line.

Shieldbearers

March in front and block enemy arrows.

1Shield Wall

Archers

Shoot before the clash and topple enemy towers.

2Arrow Rain

Giants

Smash barricades and crush armored foes.

3Ground Slam

Mages

Arcane blasts that wipe out swarms in one go.

4Arcane Blast

Golden Legion

When the bar is full, your whole army turns golden and invincible for a few seconds.

Spacegolden special

Biomes and bosses

Three realms
to conquer.

Each biome has 10 levels, its own enemies and two bosses with a weak spot. Beat the last one and that realm is yours.

The kingdom

Between battles,
a kingdom of islands.

Every bit of gold you grab on the road comes home. Each conquered realm becomes an island linked by bridges, and the defeated boss stands guard as your ally.

  • MonarchOn your first visit, you crown your king or queen and name your kingdom.
  • ConstructionBuildings that power up your troops, with builders hard at work in plain sight.
  • Endless goldYour kingdom keeps making gold even while the game is closed.
  • DefenseSkeleton hordes attack, but you always get a warning. Aim the tower arrows, send in troops and repair the wall.
