The feeling

There’s a moment when the screen fills with zombies, the blue tank maxes out and you hit the nitro. That moment is the game.

How to play

One finger.
Three lanes. No breaks.

01

Swipe to switch lanes.

Swipe sideways to dodge hazards and pick your line of fire. Swipe up to jump over holes, zombies and the boss’s shockwave.

← swipe → · ↑ jump
02

Hold, and the machine gun does the talking.

The car fires on its own with its roof launcher. Hold your finger on the road and the blue machine gun kicks in to clear the way.

hold = machine gun
03

Unleash the NITRO.

Invincible, the car blasts ahead and runs over everything. The machine gun and the nitro drink from the same tank — when to spend it is up to you.

blue tank · shared power

Garage · 13 vehicles

Pick your ride.

From the compact Rook to the Overlord, bought with whatever you collect on the road. Every car truly changes the game: more armor, more damage, more nitro. Drag to spin.

Four bosses

They don’t wait
for the horde to pass.

They show up right in the middle of it, pounding the asphalt, hurling tools and charging into your lane. From level 10 on, a mid-boss joins the final boss.

Boss · 01

Traffic Warden

Blows the whistle, writes you a ticket and shuts down your lane at the worst possible moment.

Boss · 02

Pit Specialist

Changes a tire in two seconds and throws the wrench in one.

Boss · 03

Elite Driver

Knows every curve of the road. And every one of your mistakes.

Boss · 04

Head Mechanic

His wrench is bigger than your car. Almost.

Lost? It’s not over

Welcome
to Hell.

From level 45 on, dying isn’t the end: you fall into Hell. Beat the boss down there and you’re back exactly where you left off, with everything you had. From level 50 on, you can fall twice — and the second time is a Hell of pure rage, with twice the zombies.
