The feeling

There’s a moment when the screen fills with zombies, the blue tank maxes out and you hit the nitro. That moment is the game.

How to play

One finger.
Three lanes. No breaks.

01

Swipe to switch lanes.

Swipe sideways to dodge hazards and pick your line of fire. Swipe up to jump potholes, barriers and rolling tires. On ramps, spin in the air and land straight to fill your nitro.

← swipe → · ↑ jump
02

Hold, and the machine gun does the talking.

Your car fires on its own from the roof launcher. Hold the machine gun button and it sweeps your lane. Keep the combo at ×5 and FURY kicks in: more damage and faster shots.

hold = machine gun
03

Unleash the NITRO.

Invincible, the car blasts ahead and runs over everything. The machine gun and the nitro drink from the same tank — when to spend it is up to you.

blue tank · shared power

Garage · 12 cars

Pick your ride.

From the compact Rook to the Golden, bought with the credits you rack up on the road. Each car brings more HP, more damage and faster nitro. Drag to spin.

Four bosses

They don’t wait
for the horde to pass.

They show up mid-horde, each with a signature move. A red lane warns you where the hit will land: get out of it. After a charge, the boss is dazed and takes double damage. From stage 10 on, a mid boss shows up halfway down the road.

Boss · 01

The Guard

Raises a barricade in one lane and waits for you to crash into it.

Boss · 02

Pit Boss

Rolls tires down your lane. Jump or switch lanes.

Boss · 03

The Pilot

Charges twice in a row. Dodge the first one and you still have to dodge the second.

Boss · 04

Head Mechanic

Hurls a giant wrench that blocks two lanes. Find the open one.

Lost? It’s not over

Welcome
to Hell.

From level 45 on, dying isn’t the end: you fall into Hell. Beat the boss down there and you’re back exactly where you left off, with everything you had. From level 50 on, you can fall twice — and the second time is a Hell of pure rage, with twice the zombies.
