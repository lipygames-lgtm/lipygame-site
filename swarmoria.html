The feeling

There’s a moment when you return to the object that used to be impossible — and the swarm tears it down in seconds. That moment is the game.

Nexus · permanent progress

Six mutations

Every run,
a different swarm.

Mutations are quick picks with instant impact. Stack two or three and the same level becomes a whole new game.

Replicator

More robots from every object you consume. Your swarm doubles in size before you know it.

Corrosive

Acid-tipped claws: metal comes apart way faster.

Rapid

Faster moves, faster attacks. For players who live on reflexes.

Armored

Reinforced shells: your swarm loses far fewer robots to hazards.

Magnetic

Pulls in Matter from afar. Nothing gets left behind.

Salvager

A chance at bonus resources from every object your swarm finishes taking apart.

The swarm in motion

Small, fast
and hungry.

A single robot is next to nothing. But each one rips things apart, flies and charges — and there are hundreds of them, all at once.

Titan Hunt

Too big.
Until it isn’t.

Three Titans, one per world. When the fight starts, they look impossible. Then the swarm grows, rips off their armor piece by piece and exposes the core.

  1. 01PrepareConsume the arena and grow before the first attack.
  2. 02Take apartArms, shield and legs each have their own toughness.
  3. 03SurviveThe counterattack spawns zones that wipe out robots. Get out of them.
  4. 04Devour the coreA short damage window. Make every robot count.

Nexus

The base that
grows with you.

Every run ends at the Nexus. Matter turns into permanent upgrades — and the base transforms before your eyes: a bigger Core, energy surging, new areas lighting up.

  • ReplicationStart with more robots and grow faster.
  • MobilityMore speed to move and regroup.
  • ResilienceLose fewer robots and come back stronger.
  • SalvageMore Matter and more rare finds every run.
