Save the village.Beat Morvane to it.

Morvane and his bandits are raiding the villages of the kingdom, and every villager he carries off makes him stronger. Pick a hero, race the clock and reach the villagers before he does.

The feeling

There's a moment when the ritual's red circle is already glowing, the last guard drops and you cut the group free one second before Morvane. That moment is the game.

How to play

Run. Fight.
Rescue.

01

Race through the village.

The clock starts ticking from the first second. The minimap shows where the captured villagers are held, and the score shows who's ahead: you or Morvane.

WASD, arrow keys or joystick
02

Beat the bandits.

Every group of villagers has bandits standing guard. Attack, dodge when the red warning shows up and fire off your hero's ability at just the right moment.

J or click · Space to dodge
03

Hold to free them.

With the guards down, hold until the circle fills. The villagers go free and the point is yours.

hold F · on mobile, the hand button
04

Get there before Morvane.

Morvane is out there too, heading for the groups, and he casts a ritual to carry them off. At the end of the round you duel him: every group you saved makes you stronger for the fight.

score: you × Morvane

Heroes

Three heroes,
three ways to save the day.

Each hero has their own weapon, their own ability and a rescue perk. They all level up from 1 to 10, and their ability gets stronger along the way.

RonanThe knight

Sword and shield: the strongest of the three. Frees villagers 30% faster.

Shield ChargeA short dash that knocks down every bandit in the way.

LyraThe rogue

The fastest of the three. Spots villagers 50% farther away on the minimap.

Shadow RollA long dash, then a few seconds invisible to bandits.

BrannThe frost druid

Controls the fight with ice. Every villager saved adds 2 seconds to the clock.

Frost WaveFreezes nearby bandits for 3 s.

Campaign

Seven villages,
each with a new rule.

The campaign runs across the Kingdom Map, village by village. Every villager Morvane carries off along the way makes him stronger for the final battle.

Village 01

Aldermoor Village

Where it all begins: the guards, the captured villagers, Morvane and the duel.

Village 02

Mill Village

Twice the size, a river with bridges and archers shooting from the other bank.

Village 03

Pedravale

Villagers locked inside houses. Go in and get them out.

Village 04

Brumaria

Recruits: some of the villagers you free fight at your side.

Village 05

Bonfire Village

Houses on fire, each on its own clock. Which one do you save first?

Village 06

Night of Crows

Night convoy: lead the freed villagers in a line to the bonfires.

Village 07 · final battle

Morvane's Citadel

The fight against Morvane with all three heroes together. Switch heroes on the fly with keys 1, 2 and 3.
